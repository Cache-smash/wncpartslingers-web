2. New Old Stock (NOS) Product Condition

WNCPARTSLINGERS specializes in genuine New Old Stock (NOS) automotive parts. "New Old Stock" designates parts that were manufactured originally by automotive manufacturers or period aftermarket suppliers, were never installed on a vehicle, but may have remained in warehouse or distributor storage for extended periods.

While the mechanical and functional integrity of the components is guaranteed, outer cardboard packaging, boxes, or blister cards may exhibit minor shelf wear, patina, or vintage aging consistent with authentic vintage parts storage.
