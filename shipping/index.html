Shipping & Handling Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 · Warehouse Direct Fulfillment from Hendersonville, NC

1. Order Processing & Handling Time

All verified orders are processed, packaged, and dispatched within 1 business day (24 hours) of cleared payment, Monday through Friday.

Orders placed over the weekend or on postal holidays are securely packed and dispatched the following business morning.

2. Carrier Options & Estimated Delivery Timelines

We ship nationwide across the United States using tier-one commercial carrier services with end-to-end tracking:

  • USPS Ground Advantage (Standard Delivery): Estimated delivery in 2–5 business days. Includes live tracking.
  • USPS Priority Mail (Expedited Delivery): Estimated delivery in 1–3 business days. Includes live tracking.
  • UPS Ground (Heavy & Bulky Assemblies): Estimated delivery in 2–5 business days. Includes live tracking.

3. Combined Shipping & Multi-Item Orders

Combined shipping calculates automatically at checkout for all multi-item orders. Shipping costs are assessed strictly on cumulative packaged weight, ensuring fair rates with zero handling markup.

4. Commercial Protective Packaging

Vintage hardware and New Old Stock (NOS) parts in historic manufacturer packaging receive reinforced protective materials. Every order is shipped in rigid corrugated boxes or padded bubble mailers with cushioning to safeguard against transit shock.

5. Tracking Information & Delivery Notifications

A live carrier tracking number is generated immediately when your shipping label is printed and is transmitted directly to your order confirmation email and account dashboard.
