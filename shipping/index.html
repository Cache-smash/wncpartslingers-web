Shipping & Handling Policy

Warehouse Direct from Hendersonville, North Carolina

1. Order Processing & Handling Time

All orders are dispatched within 1 business day (24 hours) of cleared payment, Monday through Friday (excluding postal holidays). Orders placed on weekends are securely packed and dispatched the following Monday morning.

2. Shipping Carriers & Delivery Estimates

We utilize reliable commercial carrier services to ensure fast, protected delivery across the United States:

  • USPS Ground Advantage (Standard): Estimated 2–5 business days delivery. Includes door-to-door tracking.
  • USPS Priority Mail (Expedited): Estimated 1–3 business days delivery.
  • UPS Ground (Heavy/Bulky Items): Estimated 2–5 business days delivery.

3. Combined Shipping & Rates

Combined shipping calculates automatically at checkout when purchasing multiple items in a single transaction. For multiple vintage items, shipping costs are assessed based on total packaged weight, ensuring you never overpay for multi-item orders.

4. Packaging Integrity & Protection

New Old Stock (NOS) parts and vintage packaging receive reinforced protective shipping. Items in original manufacturer boxes, blister cards, or vintage tins are packed with impact-resistant cushioning to prevent damage in transit.

5. Tracking & Notifications

Every order is assigned a live carrier tracking number immediately upon label creation. Tracking details are automatically synced to your order record and emailed upon dispatch.
