About WNCPARTSLINGERS

Dedicated High-Velocity E-Commerce for New Old Stock (NOS) & Vintage Automotive Components

Our Mission & Specialty

Headquartered in Hendersonville, North Carolina, WNCPARTSLINGERS specializes in cataloging, preserving, and distributing authentic New Old Stock (NOS) automotive parts, vintage restoration hardware, and direct replacement components.

We connect automotive restorers, professional mechanics, and classic vehicle enthusiasts with original manufacturer components that are out-of-production and difficult to locate through traditional retail parts channels.

Inventory Integrity & Verification

Every item cataloged in our inventory is carefully inspected, cross-referenced against factory parts manuals, and photographed in original manufacturer packaging or packaging condition.

  • Genuine NOS / OEM: Sourced from historic distributor stocks, dealership buyouts, and legacy manufacturer inventories.
  • Precision OE Cross-Referencing: Interchanged against OEM part numbers, casting marks, and vehicle fitment tables.
  • Original Packaging: We preserve original boxes, vintage header cards, and factory tags to guarantee authenticity.
  • Fast Same-Day / 24-Hour Dispatch: All in-stock items ship within 24 hours from our North Carolina warehouse.

Facility & Warehouse Operations

Our inventory management and fulfillment facility operates directly in Hendersonville, NC (Postal Code 28792). Orders are packed with impact-resistant commercial packaging to ensure vintage items arrive in factory condition.
